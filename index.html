About Me

Welcome to my portfolio! I am a robotics engineer, entrepreneur, and author with a passion for creating transformative technologies and fostering innovation. Currently pursuing a Master's degree in Robotics at the University of Maryland, I bring a strong foundation in Mechanical Engineering from Jawaharlal Nehru Technological University. As the founder and leader of Futurekrop (Agrietern Technologies), I have built an organic food brand that leverages cutting-edge technologies and a direct-to-consumer model. Our mission is to combat food waste while enhancing the livelihoods of farmers, driving sustainable growth for all stakeholders. Beyond my entrepreneurial pursuits, I am an active contributor to the global dialogue on technology and innovation as an author on Medium. Through my writing, I share insights, ideas, and experiences to inspire others and foster collective growth. I also lead a collaborative community dedicated to solving real-world problems, developing innovative ideas, and transforming them into startups. As an admin of this group, I actively engage with like-minded individuals to brainstorm, execute, and bring ventures to life. What truly drives me is the opportunity to create technology that betters the lives of people and leaves a positive mark on humanity. I'm eager to connect with like-minded individuals who share this vision. Together, let's forge the path towards a future defined by groundbreaking robotics technology.
